# Existing experience

The starting point for this redesign is a member app where pharmacy work is spread across two tabs.

## Home

Home carries more than five sections. Prescriptions appear here, but they are mixed with other member content. A person looking for a refill has to decide which blocks are tasks and which are not.

## Pharmacy

Pharmacy lists prescriptions again, organized differently from Home. The same medication can show up in both places without a shared status, last refill date, or cost.

## What a refill requires today

To refill, a member has to find the medication, figure out whether it is eligible, and then move through pharmacy and delivery choices without a single cost shown up front. The path is longer than it needs to be, and the important facts are not grouped on the medication itself.

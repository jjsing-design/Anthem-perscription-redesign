# Problem

Members need to know three things before they refill: whether they can refill now, what it will cost, and where it will be filled. In the current app those answers are split across the Home tab and the Pharmacy tab, and they sit next to content that does not require an action.

The result is a member scanning several sections to answer one question. Actionable prescriptions and marketing or educational content look similar, so status, date, and cost are easy to miss.

## What success looks like

- One place to see active prescriptions.
- A status that says whether a refill is available, still active, or already pending.
- The member cost shown before the refill starts.
- A refill that can be confirmed in three taps, with pharmacy and delivery chosen on the way.

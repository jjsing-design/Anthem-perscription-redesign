# Problem statement
Anthem users with multiple medications have a hard time understanding the status of their medications. The user can have delays in getting their medication and they can run out before their refill arrives.
## What "status" means here
What needs a refill, how and where to get it, and what happens after a request is made.
## How success is measured
This is an outcome measure, not an output measure. A member can find an eligible prescription, request a refill, and confirm its status accurately, without help.
Fewer taps is not the goal. A flow with fewer taps but more errors is worse.
Planned measurement: usability sessions with fictional healthcare scenarios, looking at task completion, errors, and hesitation. These sessions have not been run yet.

# Validation plan

Test the prototype with members, or with people walking through a member task. Use the sample sign-in. Do not treat this as a test of the live Anthem app.

## Tasks

1. Find out whether anything can be refilled, and what it costs, without opening the refill flow.
2. Refill the medication that is ready. Leave pharmacy and delivery on the defaults.
3. Start another refill, switch from pickup to home delivery, and say what the new total is before confirming.
4. Ask Sydney what can be refilled, and compare that answer with the Pharmacy list.
5. Log out and sign in again. Check that the earlier refill is no longer pending.

## What to watch

- Do they go to Pharmacy, or do they look for the list on Home?
- Do they trust the badge, or do they still open the medication to see if they can refill?
- Do they notice the cost change when they select home delivery?
- Do they understand that a disabled Refill Now is waiting on a date or on an order already in progress?

## Done when

A participant can name the refillable medication and its cost from the list, complete a refill, and correctly state the higher total after choosing home delivery.

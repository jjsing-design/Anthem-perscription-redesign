# Iteration

## First prototype

Login, Home, Pharmacy, refill confirmation, order confirmation, Claims, and a bottom nav. Three prescriptions, three pharmacies, three claims, one member. Refill Now followed the refill date. Cost was on the list and in the breakdown. Settings was present and did not open.

## Visual pass

The layout moved from a dark navy header to the reference: blue gradient, white cards, rings for deductible and out-of-pocket, and a lighter tab bar.

## Behavior pass

- Sydney is marked as AI and shows a thinking state before an answer.
- Settings opens and logs the member out, back to sign-in.
- Confirming a refill shows a loading state, then the order confirmation.
- Home delivery adds a $4.99 fee. Pickup does not. The estimated cost updates with the choice and is repeated on the order confirmation.

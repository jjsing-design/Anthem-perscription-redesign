# Case study

## Frame

Members refill prescriptions inside an app that also has to explain benefits, claims, and plan spending. The refill itself was hard to see. Prescriptions were listed in more than one place, status and cost were easy to miss, and content that did not need an action sat in the same view.

## Approach

Treat Pharmacy as the only prescription list. Show status, last refill date, and estimated cost on each medication. Let Refill Now turn on only when the date has passed and no refill is already pending. Keep Home as a front door: an AI assistant, plus shortcuts into prescriptions, claims, and the plan.

## The flow

From the list, Refill Now opens a confirm screen. The member can change pharmacy and choose pickup or home delivery. Pickup has no delivery fee. Home delivery adds $4.99 and the estimated total updates immediately. Confirm shows a loading state, then an order confirmation with that total.

## Visual direction

The interface follows a light health-app reference: blue gradient headers, white cards, spending shown as rings, and a bottom bar with a clear current tab. The goal was a calmer screen where the medication, the status, and the price read first.

## What this prototype is for

It is a portfolio piece for trying the information design and the refill path. Sydney answers from sample data. Validation is planned as task walks, not as a launched feature. Details live in the other notes in this folder.
